# Architecture Year 1 — official syllabus (NTU, Technical Engineering College Kirkuk)

Source: the owner's documents (2026-10-15): the programme catalogue "Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) – Architecture and Building Techniques, Program Catalogue 2024-2025" and the Ministry course-description forms (نموذج وصف المقرر, 2024) for the Year-1 modules. Staff names and contacts are deliberately not copied here. Weekly topics are transcribed from the course descriptions; where the source was garbled the meaning was kept, and the Arabic/English module names follow the catalogue.

## Programme facts that matter for being first
- 300 ECTS over 5 levels / 10 semesters; 30 ECTS per semester; 1 ECTS = 25 h of total student work.
- **The cumulative average is ECTS-weighted**: Σ(module mark × module ECTS) / total ECTS. So a 9-ECTS studio counts 4.5× a 2-ECTS general subject.
- Grades: A Excellent 90–100 · B Very good 80–89 · C Good 70–79 · D Satisfactory 60–69 · E Sufficient 50–59 · FX Fail (credit possible) 45–49 · F Fail 0–44. Marks are rounded at .5 (54.5 → 55, 54.4 → 54); no "near-pass" adjustments.
- Typical assessment (from the module forms): final exam 50%, mid-term 10%, the remaining 40% from quizzes, assignments, projects/lab and reports. Studios and drawing modules are assessed continuously (short quizzes, written tests, term and final exams, presentations).
